Our Celebration

About the Festival

Sri Maha Ganapathi Utsav 2026 is a celebration of devotion, tradition, unity and community spirit. Organized by Shiva Shakthi Youth, the festival brings devotees and families together to seek the blessings of Lord Ganesha and celebrate with faith, joy and togetherness.

🪔

Divine Blessings

We come together with devotion and sincere prayers to seek the blessings of Lord Ganesha for peace, prosperity and happiness.

🤝

Community Unity

The festival brings families, friends, youth and devotees together as one community in the spirit of celebration.

❤️

Seva & Service

Through seva and community activities, we strive to spread kindness, happiness and support among everyone.

🪷

Culture & Tradition

We celebrate our devotional and cultural traditions while passing their values and spirit to future generations.
